About This Study

Ecotype Comparison

Lake trout exhibit remarkable morphological diversity. This study compares Lean and Siscowet ecotypes to understand genetic and epigenetic underpinnings of their phenotypic differences.

PacBio HiFi Sequencing

High-fidelity long-read sequencing enables detection of structural variants and native DNA methylation simultaneously, providing comprehensive genomic insights.

Open Science

All data, code, and analyses are publicly available on GitHub. This browser is deployed via GitHub Pages for easy access and reproducibility.
